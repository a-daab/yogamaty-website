The Mat That Knows Its Origins

The YogaMaty is handwoven on traditional floor looms by skilled artisans in Bhaktapur, Nepal — one of the world's oldest cities, famous for its centuries-old weaving heritage. Each mat takes 6 to 8 hours to complete and carries the subtle imperfections that come only from human hands.

Natural wool and cotton fibres are selected from cooperative highland shepherds in Mustang District, hand-spun by women-led cooperatives in Pokhara Valley, and dyed with plant-based natural dyes — madder root, indigo, and turmeric — at a zero-discharge facility in Patan.

The result is a mat with exceptional natural grip, gentle cushioning, and a texture that improves with practice. It is entirely free of PVC, rubber, synthetic foam, and petroleum products.

Most importantly: every YogaMaty comes with a QR code on the label that connects you to the full, verified supply chain record for your exact production batch — including worker names and wages, environmental data, certifications, and photos from the production floor.

Why no air freight? Air shipping produces ~50× more CO₂ per unit than sea freight.
