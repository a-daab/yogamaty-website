Nepal Craftsmanship

Centuries of
Weaving Wisdom

In the Kathmandu Valley, the art of hand-loom weaving has been passed from parent to child for over 800 years. Our partnership with the Bhaktapur Artisan Cooperative means every stitch is the work of a skilled craftsperson, not a machine.

We work exclusively with women-led cooperatives. Our weavers set their own hours, choose their own workloads, and receive full benefits including healthcare and childcare support.

Sustainability

Good for Your Practice.
Good for the Planet.

From the pastures where our wool is shorn to the port where your mat sets sail, we obsess over environmental impact at every step.

Natural Materials Only

Every fibre in your YogaMaty is natural — no PVC, no rubber, no synthetic grip coatings. It's fully compostable at end of life, returning safely to the earth that made it.

Plastic-Free Packaging

Your mat arrives wrapped in recycled cotton cloth, sealed with a compostable corn-starch band. Our transparency label is printed with soy ink on FSC-certified paper. Zero plastic, full stop.

100% Carbon Offset

We use sea freight exclusively — the lowest-carbon commercial shipping method. Every remaining tonne of CO₂ from your mat's journey is offset through verified reforestation in the Nepal Himalayas.

TransparaTrade™

A Global Table of Peace for People & Planet

TransparaTrade™ is a survivor-developed global framework designed to end modern slavery and address the climate crisis simultaneously — by reforming economic systems through transparent, ethical supply chains.

The exploitation of people and the exploitation of the planet are two sides of the same crisis. TransparaTrade™ offers an immediate, unified solution to both — and the YogaMaty is its first real-world pilot.
